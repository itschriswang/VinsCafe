# Who to ring, and what to ask

Ordered by uncertainty resolved per phone call. The first six are free and
between them settle almost everything that matters.

Contact details are deliberately not written down here — they change, and the
plan should not carry a stale phone number. Search for the current ones.

---

## 1. LANDATA / a conveyancer — **do this first**

**Why:** a covenant against trade or business is fatal and nothing else matters
until you know. **Cost:** ~$20–$50.

- Order the **Certificate of Title** and the **plan of subdivision** for 10 Katupna Ct.
- Order the **instrument** for any covenant listed under encumbrances, and read the actual wording.
- Is there any restriction on carrying on a trade or business?
- Is there an owners corporation?

→ [10-title-covenants-mortgage.md](10-title-covenants-mortgage.md)

## 2. Your home insurer — **do this first too**

**Why:** the only risk here that can cost more than the house is worth. **Cost:** free.

- I am converting my garage into a registered commercial food premises. **Will you continue to insure the dwelling?**
- What happens to my premium?
- Does my current policy exclude business liability? Confirm in writing.
- If I instead garage a mobile food van here but trade elsewhere, does that change your answer?

→ [08-insurance-and-liability.md](08-insurance-and-liability.md)

## 3. Whitehorse City Council — statutory planning

**Why:** they decide whether this is allowed at all. Ask for a **pre-application discussion**. **Cost:** free.

- What **zone** and what **overlays** apply to 10 Katupna Ct?
- Is *food and drink premises* a Section 2 use in that zone here? What conditions attach?
- What **car parking rate** applies, and how would a reduction be assessed in a cul-de-sac?
- Does converting the garage create a problem with the dwelling's own required parking?
- Would you require a **traffic report**, a **waste management plan**, an **acoustic report**?
- What is the current **application fee** and realistic timeline for an advertised change of use?
- Would you expect to impose a **s173 agreement**?
- Frankly: have you approved anything like this before, and what conditions did it carry?

→ [02-planning-permit.md](02-planning-permit.md)

## 4. Whitehorse City Council — environmental health

**Why:** they decide what the garage has to become. **Cost:** free.

- Confirm the **class** for this menu. Is there any version of it that is Class 3?
- Will you **assess plans before I build**?
- Is a domestic kitchen acceptable for **any** part of this operation?
- Current **Class 2 registration fee** and renewal cycle?
- Is the **standard template Food Safety Program** acceptable here?
- Specific requirements for **hand basins, sinks, finishes, ventilation** in a converted garage?
- How do you want the **roller door opening** pest-proofed?
- If I instead register a **mobile food premises** garaged at this address, what is the process and fee?

→ [03-food-registration.md](03-food-registration.md)

## 5. Yarra Valley Water — trade waste

**Why:** one answer determines a large chunk of the fitout. **Cost:** free.

- Can a **residential-titled property** hold a food business trade waste agreement?
- What **grease arrestor** size would this menu require, and where can it physically go?
- Current **application and contract fees**?
- Is a **backflow prevention device** required, and who tests it annually?
- Does this reclassify the property's **water and sewer tariff**?

→ [05-utilities-waste.md](05-utilities-waste.md)

## 6. Your lender

**Why:** free, and a "no" changes everything. **Cost:** free.

- Does converting part of the dwelling to a registered commercial food premises **breach my loan terms**?
- Do you require **consent** for a change of use?
- Does a commercial use registered against the title change how you **classify or value** the security?

→ [10-title-covenants-mortgage.md](10-title-covenants-mortgage.md)

## 7. Fair Work Ombudsman — free

- **Which award** covers a cafe with counter service and a small seated area?
- Confirm the current **casual Level 2 Sunday rate** and **minimum engagement**.
- Current **superannuation** obligations and payment timing.
- Correct treatment of a **family member** who works regular shifts.

→ [07-employment.md](07-employment.md)

## 8. A property lawyer — paid, but only if a covenant exists

- What exactly does the covenant say, and does a cafe breach it?
- **Has the Planning Amendment (Better Decisions Made Faster) Act 2026 commenced, and does it change the s61(4) position?** This is genuinely unresolved in this plan and it matters.
- Would the covenant bar a **mobile food business garaged here but trading elsewhere**?

## 9. A registered building surveyor — paid

- Confirm the classification change and whether an **occupancy permit** is required.
- At what patron number do **patron sanitary facilities** become mandatory? Can we design to stay under **20**?
- What **fire separation** is required from the dwelling, and what happens to the internal door?
- Do **emergency lighting and exit signs** apply at this floor area?
- What is our **DDA** exposure if we provide no accessible toilet?
- What **Essential Safety Measures** will this building carry annually?

→ [04-building-and-access.md](04-building-and-access.md)

## 10. An accountant who has done hospitality — paid

- **Sole trader or company** for a business physically inside my home?
- Should I **register for GST during the build year** to claim the fitout credits?
- **Model the CGT exposure** on this house at this floor area. Actual numbers.
- Do I pass any **non-commercial loss** test, so early losses offset other income?
- Current **instant asset write-off** position for FY2026-27.

→ [06-structure-tax-gst.md](06-structure-tax-gst.md)

## 11. An insurance broker who does hospitality — paid via commission

- Full package quote: public and product liability, contents, business interruption, food contamination, income protection.
- What limit does council registration and market trading require?

## 12. Free business advice

- **Business Victoria** and the **Victorian Small Business Commission** — general guidance, dispute help.
- **Small Business Mentoring Service** — cheap, experienced mentors, often ex-operators.
- **Whitehorse City Council business support** — many councils run free advisory sessions for local businesses.
- **business.gov.au grant finder** — check for current Victorian food or small business grants. **[VERIFY]**

## The neighbours

Not a phone call, and not a formal step, but the most predictive conversation in
this whole plan. Eight to ten households, before you lodge anything, with a
coffee and an honest description of what you are proposing.

Their reaction tells you more about whether this succeeds than any of the calls
above.
