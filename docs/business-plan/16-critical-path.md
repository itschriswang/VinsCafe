# The critical path

The order matters more than the content. Almost everyone doing this spends money
on design and equipment first, and discovers the fatal problem in month six. The
sequence below front-loads every cheap way to find out that this will not work.

**Nothing in Stage 1 costs more than about $100. Do not start Stage 2 until
Stage 1 is clear.**

## Stage 0 — this week, free

- [ ] Read [01-the-verdict.md](01-the-verdict.md).
- [ ] Decide honestly: is this a **business** or a **hobby that washes its face**?
      The answer changes which alternative in [11](11-alternatives.md) is right,
      and it is the one question nobody else can answer for you.
- [ ] Look up the **zone and overlays** for 10 Katupna Ct on VicPlan. Free.

## Stage 1 — the kill switches. About $100, about a week.

Any one of these can end the garage plan. Do them **in parallel**, and do them
**before anything else**.

- [ ] **Title search + covenant instrument** via LANDATA or a conveyancer.
      ~$20–$50. Look for any restriction on trade or business.
      → [10-title-covenants-mortgage.md](10-title-covenants-mortgage.md)
- [ ] **Ring your home insurer.** "I am converting my garage into a registered
      commercial food premises. Will you still insure the dwelling, and at what
      premium?" Get the answer in writing. Free.
      → [08-insurance-and-liability.md](08-insurance-and-liability.md)
- [ ] **Ring Whitehorse statutory planning** for a pre-application discussion.
      Free. Ask the questions at the end of
      [02-planning-permit.md](02-planning-permit.md).
- [ ] **Ring Whitehorse environmental health.** Confirm the class for this menu
      and whether they will look at plans before you build. Free.
      → [03-food-registration.md](03-food-registration.md)
- [ ] **Ring Yarra Valley Water.** Can a residential property hold a food trade
      waste agreement? Free.
      → [05-utilities-waste.md](05-utilities-waste.md)
- [ ] **Ring your lender** if the property is mortgaged. Free.

**Decision gate.** If the covenant bars business use, or the insurer says no, or
planning says the parking cannot work — **stop, and go to
[11-alternatives.md](11-alternatives.md).** You will have spent under $100 to
avoid spending $57,000–$229,000.

## Stage 2 — test the demand before you build anything. 1–3 months, $3k–$15k.

Do this **even if Stage 1 was all green**. It is the only way to learn the one
thing that no permit can tell you: whether strangers will come twice.

- [ ] Register a **temporary food premises** through FoodTrader.
- [ ] Do **four to six market days**. Coffee and matcha only — Class 3, minimal
      compliance.
- [ ] Measure the four numbers from [14-operations.md](14-operations.md):
      transactions per day, average transaction value, waste percentage, and how
      you feel on Monday.
- [ ] Open the Instagram. Start the audience now; it takes longer than the
      building work.

**Decision gate.** Compare your real transactions per hour against the scenarios
in [13-the-numbers.md](13-the-numbers.md). If you cannot reach the **lean** case
of 8 transactions an hour at a market — where the foot traffic is handed to you —
you will not reach it in a cul-de-sac.

## Stage 3 — the professionals. 1–2 months, $5k–$20k.

Only once Stages 1 and 2 are clear.

- [ ] Engage a **town planner** who has done change-of-use in Whitehorse.
- [ ] Engage a **registered building surveyor** — before the architect, before
      the builder. Ask the [04](04-building-and-access.md) questions, especially
      the 20-person threshold.
- [ ] Engage an **accountant** who has done hospitality. Model the **CGT
      exposure** properly. → [06](06-structure-tax-gst.md)
- [ ] **Talk to the neighbours.** Before lodging. The highest-yield hour in the
      entire project. → [09](09-neighbours.md)
- [ ] Get an **insurance broker** to quote the real package.

## Stage 4 — the application. 3–9 months, mostly waiting.

- [ ] Lodge the **planning permit** application, with the patron cap and hours
      volunteered as conditions.
- [ ] Submit **food premises plans** to environmental health in parallel — they
      will change the design, and it is far cheaper before the building permit.
- [ ] Public notice, objections, response.
- [ ] Decision. If refused: VCAT, or
      [11-alternatives.md](11-alternatives.md).

**Do not order equipment during this stage.** It is the most tempting moment and
the most expensive mistake.

## Stage 5 — build. 2–4 months, $50k–$200k.

- [ ] Building permit issued by the surveyor.
- [ ] Trade waste agreement and grease arrestor.
- [ ] Fitout, in the order the surveyor and the EHO tell you.
- [ ] Backflow device, plumbing compliance certificates.
- [ ] Occupancy permit.
- [ ] Food Act registration, Food Safety Program, Food Safety Supervisor
      certificate.
- [ ] Insurance bound. Commercial waste contract. Business name, ABN, GST.
- [ ] Equipment, POS, EFTPOS, opening stock.

## Stage 6 — open. Quietly.

- [ ] **Friends-and-family service at full pace**, unpaid, to find the
      bottlenecks before strangers do.
- [ ] Week 1: deliberately under-promote. Forty customers served well beats a
      hundred served badly.
- [ ] Records from day one — temperatures, waste, sales.
      → [14-operations.md](14-operations.md)

## The shape of it

| Stage | Time | Cost | Cumulative risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1 | 1 week | ~$100 | Trivial |
| 2 | 1–3 months | $3k–$15k | Recoverable — you own a cart |
| 3 | 1–2 months | $5k–$20k | Sunk if refused |
| 4 | 3–9 months | included | Sunk if refused |
| 5 | 2–4 months | $50k–$200k | Committed |
| **Total** | **9–18 months** | **$57k–$229k** | |

Nine to eighteen months, and the great majority of the money is in the last
stage. That is a good shape: the expensive commitment comes last, and there are
four decision gates before it where the honest answer might be "not like this".

Take those gates seriously. The plan is not "get to Stage 5". The plan is to find
out, as cheaply as possible, whether Stage 5 is worth reaching — and to have a
real business running at Stage 2 either way.
